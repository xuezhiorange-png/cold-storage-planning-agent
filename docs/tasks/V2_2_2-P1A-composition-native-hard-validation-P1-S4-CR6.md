## Design

The material chain is read from `process_graph().flows`; its hard edge checks
are read from the same graph's `must_adjacencies`. For each candidate role and
authoritative shape, the forward probe forms the finite union of composition
domain, existing Access-aware, generic physical-event, and MUST-face origins.
Before charging geometric expansions, it removes only origins that fail the
existing exact positive-length MUST edge predicate against any already placed
MUST neighbor. The unchanged site/obstacle/overlap, MUST, composition-side,
projection, and partial-intent checks remain in the normal path.

The recursive forward probe finds the first supported successor and continues
along the existing chain; exhaustive finite-domain exhaustion is required for
a negative result. Budget exhaustion remains UNKNOWN and cannot prune. A
capacity witness records the exact parent partial hash, composition identity
and signature, bank sign, predecessor/successor, bounds, shape identity, and
finite-domain provenance. It is invocation-local, non-authoritative, and
revalidated as the first primary candidate; normal anchors remain available
after rejection. Parent-hash mismatch prevents reuse after geometry changes.

Per-shape origins are materialized before the role's expansion loop so the
probe funnel can account for the complete finite domain, including candidates
not expanded after a shared-budget stop.

each covering all 13 scheduled attempts. Both used 55,964 nodes (48,949
primary plus 7,015 forward-check), with attempt allocations totaling 60,000;
both stayed below the frozen global ceiling and had valid node accounting.
Each attempt schedule covered 5 Linear, 4 Central, and 4 Spine attempts across
7 Access-aware and 6 S3-compatibility order lanes. No complete candidate was
constructed, so Access candidate assessment, Truck validation, and P2D were
not reached.

`S3_COMPATIBILITY_ORDER`) reached 6 simultaneously placed roles, versus 5 in

returned `UNKNOWN_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. Seven Coating partials were checked for
Finished capacity: six exhausted their finite domains with no hard-valid
successor; the seventh remained UNKNOWN because 127 of 1,157 CR5-compatible
Finished origins were not expanded before the shared slice ended. The
accounting is complete: 1,272 finite origins = 1,272 MUST-compatible; 1,157

Finished-to-Shipping capacity check was not reached. These results are scoped
to the current finite search domain and do not establish global infeasibility.

successor-capacity sequence, forward-check sequence, forward-witness sequence,
successor-witness sequence, candidate hashes (both empty), best candidate
