## Proposed scoped proof mechanism

One variable per HARD-component role remains mandatory. Necessary origin boxes
are propagated using actual fixed MUST neighbors and conservative shared-face
arc projections. Axis-aligned rectangular hard obstacles may be subtracted
exactly in integer origin space; nonrectangular obstacle bounding boxes must
not be subtracted as if they were obstacles. Polygon validity remains the exact
predicate's responsibility. Resource guards relax/discard propagation rather
than inventing empty-space proofs.

Within the existing positive probe, authoritative shape order is unchanged.
Remaining necessary space size only breaks variable ties when at least two MUST
neighbors are fixed; fixed-neighbor/shape-count/degree priority remains first.
Exact equal-axis integer strip coalescing reduces representation fragmentation
without deleting any integer point. All four box corners plus authority-derived
events remain advisory witness proposals. A positive certificate still requires
the complete unified assignment to pass every existing physical, MUST,
non-overlap and intent predicate. Nonempty necessary space is not a positive
certificate; unsuccessful finite exploration is not a negative certificate.

The existing negative verifier and coverage contract remain unchanged. The
positive propagation result alone cannot authorize pruning. Parent/sibling
states are immutable and authorities plus partial geometry plus revision bind
every newly revalidated certificate. No joint witness is exported as an
unanchored production placement candidate.

Frozen resources: evaluation cap 256, origin-event cap 1024, placement budget
60000. Placement/application code, scheduler, zone order, families, P0/P1,
P2 public policy, R1 site authority, engineering authorities and Access/Truck/
P2D remain frozen. No standalone business routing validation is authorized.

and negative-probe resource guards cannot authorize rejection. R4 intentionally
does not promote incomplete positive-space propagation into a new negative
certificate. It also does not raise evaluation/event limits to hide the gap.

intersections and 8,420,637 rectangular-obstacle subtractions; these are query
